# Selection evidence

Checked 14 October 2026. Family: loyalty-program, unused in the rebuild registry. This differs from CRM, paid membership administration, trade promotions and sales rebates: the primary records are loyalty point lots and non-cash member rewards. The preceding run built data-centre operations, which was excluded.

Salesforce Loyalty Management Starter has a published USD list price of US$20,000 per organisation per month billed annually, or US$240,000 annually. No EDNA customer invoice or customer use was confirmed. Local customer-text search found no loyalty programme evidence. The Australian Salesforce site markets this product, but that is not evidence that a particular NZ or AU small business pays for it.

Scores in charter order: bill 5, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 2, search demand 3, one-session buildable 4, import 4. Total 23/30. The bill score uses a public annual list price. The search score uses visible alternatives results, not measured keyword volume. Buyer fit stays low because customer usage is unconfirmed. Import is 4 because standard member CSV and history export exist but operational points require reconciliation.

Other candidates checked: IntelligenceBank digital asset management needs a quote and media workflows, with no verified five-figure customer bill in this run. Forms On Fire is dominated by mobile field collection, which needs a front end. Cflow is already covered by the workflow-approvals family. None passed all selection filters ahead of this candidate, so no speculative annual invoice was assigned to them.
